1 Understand the idea

Purpose / What does this do?

In your own words, what is this idea trying to accomplish?

How does this work?

Briefly describe how you understand it functioning.

2 Existing landscape + gap

What does this remind you of?

Products, services, systems, tools, policies, behaviors, research, or other analogues.

Gap / Difference

What does this add, change, combine, improve, or leave unresolved?

3 Who, where + when?

Beachhead / Initial users

Who should probably use, test, buy, support, or encounter this first?

Who is this for / not for?

Who benefits, pays, decides, is affected, unaffected, or may resist?

Context of use

Where is this best used? When, how often, and under what conditions?

4 Initial reaction + personal demand

What do you like about it now?

What do you not like about it now?

Would you personally want or use this?

Why?

If it worked as imagined, would you recommend it?

To whom?

5 Features + friction

Features / Missing

What seems essential, useful, unnecessary, confusing, or missing?

Friction / Adoption barriers

What could make someone hesitate to try, adopt, buy, approve, recommend, or keep using it?

6 Scope, boundaries + limitations

Where does this fit, and where does it stop fitting?

What is it good for or not good for? What boundaries or limitations do you already see or suspect? Consider users, geography, scale, cost, technology, regulation, time, context, expertise, or dependencies.

7 Failure points + assumptions

How could this fail?

Consider technical, behavioral, financial, operational, social, institutional, legal, or other failure modes.

What needs to be true?

What assumptions, missing pieces, stakeholders, resources, or conditions does the idea depend on?

8 Evidence + validation

What seems supported, what needs testing, and what could change your mind?

Identify convincing claims, claims needing evidence, and tests or evidence that would materially change your view.

9 Market, business + scale

How far could this realistically go?

Consider need, audience, willingness to pay or support, costs, funding, partners, operations, distribution, regulation, maintenance, replication, and scaling limits.

10 Change it + next step

If you could change one, two, three… things, what would you change?

What should happen next?

11 Core / Invariant

What should not change?

What about this idea should be preserved even if everything else changes? What makes it fundamentally this idea?
